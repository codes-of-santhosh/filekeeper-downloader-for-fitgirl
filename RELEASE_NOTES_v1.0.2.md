# 🚀 v1.0.2 — Drag-to-Select, Range Highlighting & UI Enhancements

This release adds **bulk drag selection**, **keyboard Spacebar toggling**, and **centered UI branding** to make selecting parts from large queues effortless.

---

### 🌟 What's New in v1.0.2

- 🖱️ **Drag-to-Select & Range Highlighting:**
  - Click and drag your mouse across rows to highlight any range of files.
  - Hold `Shift` + click to select continuous ranges, or `Ctrl` + click to select separate files.

- ⌨️ **Instant Spacebar Toggle:**
  - Press the **Spacebar** while files are highlighted to toggle all selected checkboxes at once.

- 🔘 **Check & Uncheck Highlighted Action Buttons:**
  - Added dedicated **Check Highlighted** and **Uncheck Highlighted** buttons for fast bulk actions.

- 🎯 **Centered Hero Branding:**
  - Refined banner artwork with perfectly centered app icon alignment.
